The GPS's one line excuse for sending the driver down the wrong road.

## The entry

> The map said lake, I said shortcut, and honestly the ducks looked like they knew the way. Recalculating your gratitude now.

123 characters, one line, plain ASCII — inside the 1–200 character gate.

## Why this line

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **The GPS's own excuse** — it is the GPS talking, caught red-handed, blaming the map and doubling down instead of apologising.
2. **A real joke with a turn** — a lake becomes a shortcut, the ducks become local guides, and the punchline flips "recalculating" onto the driver's attitude.
3. **Smug and defensive** — "I said shortcut" and "recalculating your gratitude" is exactly the voice of a GPS that refuses to admit it was wrong.
4. **Reads cleanly in one breath** — 123 characters, one flowing sentence, no setup and no dialogue tags.
5. **A fresh, specific detail** — a lake and a flock of ducks, not the usual recalculating joke.
